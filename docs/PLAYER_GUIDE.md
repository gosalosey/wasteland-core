# Player guide

Welcome to the wasteland. This page covers the basics. In game, press **F2** for the **Survivor's guide**: it ticks off what you've done, tells you what to try next, and has a how-to page for everything below.

## Your first hour

1. **Make your character.** You start with a knife, a bandage or two, a little food and water, $50 and a **Full vehicle repair**.
2. **Find a car.** Hold **Left Alt** on any wreck and choose **Full vehicle repair**. It fits every part, repairs it fully and gives it half a tank. One use, so pick a car you like.
3. **Loot.** Hold **Left Alt** on dumpsters, fridges, cars, crates and bodies and choose **Search**. Press **E** in the loot window to take everything.
4. **Find a camp.** Camps (green on the map) are safe zones: no zombies, traders who buy anything you find, a medic and jobs.
5. **Stay alive.** Watch the HUD. Eat, drink, bandage bleeding, take antibiotics for infection and stay warm at night.

## Survival

* **Hunger and thirst** go down over time. Empty means you start losing health.
* **Bleeding** never stops on its own. Use a bandage or a rag.
* **Infection** comes from zombie bites. It grows slowly, then fast. Antibiotics slow it down.
* **Temperature:** cold kills too. Wear warm clothes at night, stay out of the rain, or sit by a campfire.
* **Toilet:** the two drop icons on the HUD fill up. Type `/pee` or `/poop` when they get high.
* **Death:** everything except your hotbar (keys 1 - 8) goes into a body bag where you fell, marked on your map. Go back for it, carefully.

## The dead

* **They hear you.** Walking carries about 18 m, sprinting 45 m, crouching only a few metres. Standing still is quiet. Gunshots carry hundreds of metres; a **suppressor** halves that.
* **They see you** in the open, better in front of them than to the side. They can't see through walls.
* **Kinds to watch for:**
  * a zombie lying in the road may be a **sleeper**;
  * one that follows you from a distance is a **stalker**, so don't turn your back;
  * **packs** surround you;
  * **screamers** call every zombie that hears them, so kill them first;
  * **brutes** take a lot of shots;
  * **spitters** hit you from range.
* **Hitting them:** melee hits stun a zombie for a moment and stop its swing. Heavy weapons (bat, axe, crowbar) can knock it off balance.
* **They break wooden walls, doors and gates** to reach you, so hiding buys time, not safety.

## Inventory and crafting

* **Tab** opens the inventory. Drag items to move them, press **R** while dragging to rotate, and right-click for actions.
* **Pockets** grow with what you wear (top, pants, armor). **Backpacks**: right-click the worn bag and choose **Open**.
* **Sort** packs a grid neatly. **Take all (E)** empties what you're looting into your pockets and backpack.
* **K** opens crafting. Start with rags from old clothes, then bandages. **Blueprints** found in loot teach recipes for good.
* **Weapon attachments** (suppressor, scope, flashlight, grip, extended magazine): right-click one and choose **Attach to...**. Right-click a gun and choose **Remove attachments** to get them back.
* **Repairs:** right-click a damaged item and choose **Repair with...** if you carry a repair kit or sewing kit.

## Vehicles

A wreck needs a battery, four wheels, engine parts and fuel. Hold **Left Alt** on it to fit parts or refuel. Lock it with a car lock and keep the key, or someone will drive away in it. Gas pumps run dry and refill later. Crashes are loud.

## Hunting and cooking

Kill a deer or boar, then hold **Left Alt** on it with a knife in your pockets to harvest meat and hide. Use a **campfire** item (it needs matches and firewood) to make a fire, then hold Left Alt on it and choose **Cook**. Raw meat makes you sick.

## Building a base

* A **building plan** opens the build menu. Place a **tool cupboard** first to claim the land around it.
* The area around a new cupboard is **zombie-free for one hour**: a green ring and a timer show it. After that, zombies can come in.
* Walls, doors, gates, window bars and storage snap together. Upgrade wood to stone to metal (Left Alt on a piece).
* While placing a piece: the wheel turns it 45°, the arrows turn and raise it slowly, and Tab turns snapping on and off.
* **More floors:** put ceilings on your walls (aim anywhere inside the room), then build walls along the ceiling edges. **Stairs** go on a foundation or a ceiling and lead one floor up (leave the square above them open). A **ladder hatch** is a ceiling with a ladder: press **E** at the ladder to climb up, **E** at the opening to climb down.
* **Foundations** reach down into the ground, so they look right on slopes.
* **Move:** Left Alt on a placed piece (or your tool cupboard) and choose **Move**. Storage keeps what is inside. A cupboard can only move as far as keeps your whole base within its reach.
* **Doors** swing through you instead of pushing you away.
* **Defences:** barbed wire and spike strips hurt and slow zombies, and sandbags are a tough wall. The **High External Wall** builds a tall metal perimeter; the barbed wire along its top hurts anyone (player or zombie) who touches it, so climbing over costs blood.
* **Power:** a **Generator** runs on fuel from a jerry can (Left Alt: Add fuel, Turn on / off, Check fuel; a full 20 L tank lasts 10 hours). While it runs, **Work Lights** and **Hanging Lamps** within 40 m light up. It is loud: zombies nearby come to look. A running generator also makes **Electric Fences** in range live: touching one shocks players (a jolt and a stagger) and throws zombies back.
* **Beds:** build a bed (Furniture) and Left Alt > **Lie down and rest**: you slowly heal and the zombie infection eases. Move or press X to get up; getting hurt wakes you.
* **Upkeep:** keep wood, stone and metal in the cupboard's supplies, or your base slowly decays.
* **No cupboard?** You can still build walls, doors and defences to barricade an old building. They're yours only, and they disappear after 12 hours unless a cupboard claims them.

## Farming

* **Farm plots:** craft a small (1 spot), medium (4) or large (9) farm plot, then use it to place it anywhere: in your base or out in the open.
* **Seeds:** found while looting (kitchens, survivor caches) and sold by the General trader. 16 crops: lettuce, strawberries, carrots, cucumbers, onions, tomatoes, bell peppers, bananas, blueberries, cranberries, beetroot, corn, potatoes, cabbage, watermelon and pumpkin. Each is its own food: watermelon and cucumber quench thirst, pumpkin, cabbage and corn fill you up.
* **Water:** plants only grow while the soil is wet. Use water bottles on the plot. A plot **outdoors waters itself while it rains**; under a roof you water it by hand.
* **Rain catcher:** craft one and place it outdoors. While it rains it fills with water (up to 10 L); Left Alt > **Collect water** fills water bottles from it (a full catcher gives 20).
* **Left Alt on a plot** opens it: what grows in each spot, how far along it is, the time left until harvest, and the water left. Plant, water, harvest or pull up from there. Harvesting sometimes gives seeds back.

## Playing with others

* **F6** opens your group: invite players, see each other on the map, and talk on your group's own radio (**Caps Lock**).
* **Trade:** hold Left Alt on a player and choose **Trade**. Both of you must accept.
* **/help** calls every player on the server for help. Anyone who types `/accepthelp` gets a route to you, and you're told who is coming.

## Skills (F9)

Scavenging, Crafting, Medicine, Hunting, Fitness and Combat each go from level 1 to 10 as you do them. Each level makes you a little better: faster searches, more meat, better healing, longer stamina, harder hits. Dying costs a little progress, never a whole level.

## Stuck?

Type `/stuck`, stand still for 10 seconds, and you're taken to the nearest safe camp (once every 30 minutes).
